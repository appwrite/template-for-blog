## Why a Digital Detox Matters

In a hyper-connected world, constant notifications and screen time can drain your mental energy. Stepping away from screens—even briefly—can improve focus, sleep, and overall happiness.

## Simple Ways to Disconnect

Try setting screen-free hours, turning off non-essential notifications, or swapping phone time for reading or outdoor activities. A little disconnection can lead to a better connection with yourself.
