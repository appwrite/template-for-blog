## The Key to Productivity

Time management isn’t about doing more—it’s about doing what matters. The ability to prioritize tasks effectively determines success.

## Practical Techniques That Work

Setting clear goals, avoiding multitasking, and using time-blocking methods can help structure your day better. A well-planned schedule reduces stress and maximizes output.
