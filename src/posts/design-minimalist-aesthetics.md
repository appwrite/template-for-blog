## Why Minimalism Works

Clean lines, ample white space, and simple color palettes create a visual experience that feels effortless and timeless. Removing unnecessary elements allows the essential ones to shine.

## Applying Minimalism

Whether in web design, architecture, or branding, a minimalist approach enhances clarity and usability. Simplicity is powerful.
