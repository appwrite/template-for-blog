## Designing for the User

Great UX isn’t about what looks cool—it’s about how intuitive and seamless an experience feels. Human-centered design puts the user first at every stage.

## The Future of UX

From accessibility to AI-driven personalization, the next wave of UX will focus on inclusivity and adaptive experiences.
