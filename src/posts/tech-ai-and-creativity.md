## AI’s Role in Creative Fields

From generating art to composing music, AI is proving to be a powerful creative tool. But while it can enhance human creativity, it cannot fully replace originality and emotion.

## The Collaboration Between AI and Artists

Many creators are now using AI to brainstorm ideas, enhance productivity, and push artistic boundaries. The best results come when humans and AI work together rather than against each other.
