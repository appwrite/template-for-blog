## How Color Affects Perception

From branding to user interfaces, color choice impacts how people feel and interact with designs. Red creates urgency, blue evokes trust, and green represents growth.

Studies show that color influences consumer behavior. Brands carefully choose colors to evoke emotions—think of fast-food chains using red and yellow to stimulate appetite.

## Using Color Effectively

Balancing contrast, accessibility, and cultural significance makes a design more impactful. Poor color contrast can make content hard to read, while culturally inappropriate colors may send unintended messages.

Designers who master color psychology can create visuals that are not just appealing but also functional and meaningful.
