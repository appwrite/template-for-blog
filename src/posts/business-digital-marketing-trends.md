## What’s Changing in Marketing?

Consumers expect more personalized, data-driven experiences. AI is enabling hyper-targeted ads, chatbots are handling customer support, and influencer marketing continues to rise.

## Staying Ahead

To stay relevant, brands must leverage automation, social commerce, and interactive content. The future of marketing is engagement-driven.
